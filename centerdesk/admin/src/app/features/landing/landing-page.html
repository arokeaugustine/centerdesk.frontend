Product

Everything a support team needs. Nothing it has to babysit.

Built for teams answering customers across Nigeria and beyond — where the inbox is busy and half the conversations happen on WhatsApp.

One inbox for every email and WhatsApp chat

Email threads and WhatsApp conversations land in the same queue, each with its full history. Your team works one list, in one place, whichever way the customer wrote in.

  • Reply with photos, PDFs and voice notes — on either channel
  • Leave internal notes your customer never sees
  • Always see how much of WhatsApp’s 24-hour reply window is left

AI that answers — and knows when to stay quiet

Switch AI on for the categories or mailboxes you choose. It answers only from your own knowledge base and solved tickets, sends the reply when it’s confident, and hands everything else to a person.

  • Every resolved ticket can teach it something new
  • When it holds back, the ticket says why
  • Customers can always reach a human by replying

Promises kept, shift after shift

Set response and resolution targets for each kind of request. CenterDesk pauses the clock while you wait on the customer, warns you before a ticket breaches, and moves work along when a shift ends.

  • New tickets go to whoever is on shift with the lightest load
  • Upload a month of shifts from one CSV file
  • Escalate to a resolution team without losing the thread
